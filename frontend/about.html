About Sentiverse AI

Unified customer feedback intelligence for sentiment, insight, and action

What this project does

Sentiverse AI analyzes customer review text to uncover sentiment, key product aspects, emotion signals, sarcasm risk, and suspicious review patterns. The app turns unstructured feedback into a structured dashboard and clear summaries that teams can act on.

Recommended technology stack

  • Auth: Supabase Auth for email/password signup, login, password recovery, and session management.
  • Database: Supabase Postgres with row-level security for account-owned aggregate analysis history.
  • Backend: FastAPI with Python NLP models.

Uploaded review files and individual review text are analyzed locally by the app and are not uploaded to Supabase.
